How the roofing cost estimate is calculated

This calculator is a planning tool, not a contractor quote. It uses national U.S. component ranges and does not claim to know a ZIP-code-specific contractor bid.

Formula

For supported materials, the calculator separates material, installation labor, and optional tear-off:

material cost = roof area × (1 + waste %) × material rate

labor cost = roof area × labor rate

tear-off cost = roof area × tear-off rate (only when selected)

total = material + labor + tear-off

The “Typical” number is the midpoint of the calculated Low and High planning totals. It is not claimed to be a market quote.

The national estimate does not add separate adjustments for location, roof pitch, story height, permits, decking repairs, gutters, skylights, or hidden structural damage. Local contractor quotes are the best source for project-specific factors.

What the estimate does not know

  • Your contractor’s overhead, profit, insurance, crew productivity, or scheduling constraints.
  • Local permit fees, code upgrades, ice/water shield requirements, or disposal rules.
  • Decking damage hidden under the existing roof.
  • Exact valley, dormer, chimney, skylight, flashing, and access complexity.
  • A state-by-state price multiplier. The calculator uses national ranges rather than claiming a local adjustment without a documented source.

For a purchase decision, compare multiple written contractor bids and confirm exactly what each bid includes.
