Privacy Policy

Roofing Calculator is designed to collect as little personal information as practical while you use its planning tools.

1. Calculator inputs

Roof area, material selections, waste percentages, and other calculator inputs are processed in your browser by the calculator code. Roofing Calculator does not send those calculator inputs to an application database.

2. Website hosting

This website is hosted on Vercel. Vercel may process technical information needed to deliver and protect the site under its own privacy terms.

Read Vercel's Privacy Policy.

3. Cookies, analytics, and advertising

Roofing Calculator does not use Google Analytics, Google AdSense, or first-party tracking cookies at this time.

If the site's data practices materially change, this policy will be updated as required by applicable law.

4. Email

If you email us, we receive the information you choose to include in your message and your email address so we can read and respond to your request. Do not send payment-card details, government identification numbers, insurance documents, or other sensitive personal information by ordinary email.

Privacy questions can be sent to sewang262@gmail.com.

5. Roofing quote requests

This site does not operate a roofing-contractor quote request form. No contact information is collected for contractor matching through the calculators.

6. External links

Our pages link to source publishers, manufacturers, government websites, and other third parties. Their websites have their own privacy practices, which we do not control.

7. Children

This website is intended for adults planning roofing projects and is not directed to children under 13.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our data practices change. The date at the top of this page shows the latest published revision.
