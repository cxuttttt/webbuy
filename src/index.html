Roofing cost calculator FAQ

Is this a contractor quote?

No. It is a planning calculator. A site inspection and written contractor proposal are still required for a binding price.

What is a roofing square?

A roofing square equals 100 square feet of roof area. Our Methodology page cites the GAF source used for this definition.

Why does the calculator show a range?

Actual roofing bids vary by labor market, material, roof access, pitch, tear-off scope, permit requirements and project complexity.
