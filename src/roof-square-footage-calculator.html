How this calculator works

If you know the measured roof surface area, the calculator uses that value directly. For a simple rectangular footprint, it can estimate sloped area by multiplying footprint area by the slope factor derived from the rise/run triangle.

For irregular roofs, plane-by-plane measurement is more reliable.
